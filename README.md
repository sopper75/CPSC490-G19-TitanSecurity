## Project summary

Current radar systems are expensive and cumbersome. Radar Over WiFi (ROW) turns ordinary 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi signals into radar by analyzing how those signals bounce off flying objects. The system will provide a low-cost, early-warning mesh that detects and tracks drones in real time as they move through a field of Wi-Fi transmitters and receivers.
